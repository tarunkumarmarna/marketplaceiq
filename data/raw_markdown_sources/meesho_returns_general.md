# Meesho Return & Refund Policy — General

## Standard Return Window
Meesho's own legal policy page states products are usually returnable/exchangeable within 7 days of delivery — narrower than Flipkart's 7–30 day range or Amazon's 30-day default. This is consistent with Meesho's positioning as a value/reseller-driven marketplace with thinner per-item margins than Flipkart or Amazon.

## Return vs. RTO Distinction
Meesho separates two return categories:
- **RVP (Return Post-Delivery):** the customer received the item and is returning it.
- **RTO (Return to Origin):** the item was never delivered and is returned to the seller's warehouse in transit.
Sellers are charged a return shipping fee (weight-based) for RVP returns but are not charged additional shipping fees for RTO shipments.

## Instant Refund Mechanism
For Cash on Delivery orders meeting eligibility conditions, Meesho offers an "Instant Refund" — triggered within 4 hours of the courier marking the item as picked up. The refund reflects in the customer's bank account within 3–7 business days, or within 2 business days if credited as Meesho Balance instead.

## Non-Returnable Categories
Personal care products and perishables are explicitly listed as non-returnable on the product listing; sellers must mark these categories correctly at listing time to avoid disputed return requests.

## Condition & Data Requirements
Warranty cards (where applicable) must be present at return, or the return is rejected. Electronic devices must have personal information removed and be reset before return, matching the same practice used across Flipkart and Amazon.

## Seller-Side Claims Process
If a seller disputes a return/RTO outcome, they can raise a claim via the Supplier Panel's "Return/RTO & Exchange" support section. Approved compensation is settled to the seller's bank account within 7 working days.
