# GST Registration & Invoicing Requirements for Marketplace Sellers

## Mandatory Registration, No Exemption
Unlike standalone sellers who enjoy a turnover-based GST exemption (₹40 lakh for goods, ₹20 lakh in northeastern/special-category states), sellers operating through an e-commerce operator like Flipkart, Amazon, or Meesho must register for GST regardless of turnover, per Section 24 of the CGST Act. Some platforms (e.g., Meesho) allow very small sellers to operate under an "Enrollment ID" in place of a full GSTIN when annual turnover is below ₹40 lakh, but this is a platform-level accommodation, not a change to the underlying law.

## Documents Needed for GST Registration
- PAN card (business or proprietorship)
- Aadhaar card (identity verification)
- Proof of business address (utility bill, rent agreement, or NOC)
- Bank account details
- Digital Signature Certificate (mandatory for companies/LLPs)

## Registration Process (GST Portal)
1. Navigate to Services > Registration > New Registration on the GST portal.
2. Select taxpayer type and enter legal name (must match PAN exactly), PAN, state, email, and mobile.
3. Verify via OTP; a Temporary Reference Number (TRN) is issued.
4. Complete the application, upload documents.
5. An Application Reference Number (ARN) is generated for tracking.
6. On approval, the GST Registration Certificate and GSTIN are issued.

## Linking GSTIN to Marketplace Accounts
Once issued, the GSTIN must be uploaded to each marketplace's seller dashboard (e.g., Amazon Seller Central under Settings > Account Info > Tax Information) before the seller account is activated. Platforms verify GSTIN validity and will deactivate or block an account if the GSTIN is missing, invalid, or mismatched with registered business details.

## Ongoing Filing Obligations
Sellers must file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns (monthly under the regular scheme, quarterly under QRMP), even in months with zero sales (NIL filing). The e-commerce operator separately files GSTR-8 to report TCS collected on the seller's behalf; this auto-populates the seller's GSTR-2A with the TCS credit.
