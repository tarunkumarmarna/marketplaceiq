# Flipkart Return & Refund Policy — General

## Category-Based Return Windows
Flipkart's return window depends on the product category, and the specific window for a given item is shown on that item's own product page — sellers and buyers should treat the product-page window as authoritative over any general rule. Broadly:
- Fashion & lifestyle (clothing, shoes, accessories): 7–10 day refund/exchange window; intimate wear and swimwear are typically excluded.
- Electronics & mobiles: often a shorter window, commonly a 7-day replacement-only policy for many branded products (rather than a refund), sometimes requiring service-center inspection before replacement.
- Large appliances & furniture: 7–10 days post-installation/delivery, frequently requiring an engineer's inspection report.
- Refurbished/used items: typically 7-day replacement-only, subject to quality check.
- Certain categories (some electronics, perishables, hygiene products, customized items): non-returnable entirely.

## Cancellation & Refunds
If Flipkart cancels an order, the customer is not charged, and any payment already made is refunded within 5–7 business days. For prepaid orders, refund initiation is typically fast (often same-day), but full bank credit can still take up to 5–7 working days depending on the bank.

## Condition Requirements
Returned items must be in original condition with all original packaging, tags, manuals, accessories, and warranty cards intact. Missing accessories, broken seals/holograms, or signs of use can disqualify a return or reduce it to a partial refund.

## Troubleshooting Before Replacement (Electronics)
For electronics disputes, Flipkart may first attempt online/phone troubleshooting. If no defect is confirmed or diagnosed within 7 days of delivery, the customer is directed to the brand's own service center for further resolution rather than a Flipkart-managed return.
