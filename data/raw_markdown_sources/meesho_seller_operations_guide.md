# Meesho Seller Operations Guide (Onboarding, Listing, Shipping SLA)

> Synthesized from multiple public sources — Meesho's onboarding is a supplier-panel signup flow (supplier.meesho.com) rather than a single downloadable policy document. Facts below are cross-verified across at least two independent sources where possible.

## Onboarding & Registration
1. Register at supplier.meesho.com with PAN, GSTIN (or Enrollment ID for sellers under the ₹40 lakh turnover threshold — Meesho's own accommodation, not a change in the underlying GST law), bank details, and product catalog readiness.
2. Registration is generally faster than Flipkart or Amazon, with same-day account approval commonly reported.
3. Upload GSTIN certificate, PAN card, cancelled cheque/bank statement, and address proof; blurry or low-quality scans are a common rejection reason.
4. Meesho takes a smaller commission than Flipkart/Amazon (reported comparisons suggest sellers keep close to 100% of sale price minus actual shipping/payment/return costs, versus roughly 15–25% commission on Flipkart/Amazon) — this reflects Meesho's reseller-network positioning.

## Listing Guidelines
- **Image requirements are the strictest of the three platforms:** pure white background, product filling roughly 70% of frame, no logos or watermarks (unlike Flipkart, which permits brand logos), minimum 1000×1000 pixel canvas.
- Meesho runs duplicate-image detection that flags images cross-posted unedited from Amazon or Flipkart listings — sellers need meaningful variation (crop, background tone, canvas) for a fresh catalog entry to go live.
- A size chart is mandatory for most fashion sub-categories (kurtis, tops, sarees, footwear, kidswear); missing or incorrect size charts increase returns and can trigger listing rejection.
- Common rejection causes: poor image quality, wrong category placement, misleading descriptions, fake MRP, duplicate listings, unauthorized branded items.
- Sellers can edit title, description, price, stock, and images at any time, but major edits may trigger a re-review.

## Shipping SLA & Performance Tracking
- Meesho's standard shipping SLA is **2–3 days** from order receipt to dispatch, shown in the seller's Supplier Panel for each order batch (the panel is the operative source of truth, not a single fixed public number).
- Meesho tracks a "Ready to Ship" timeline metric, the Meesho-specific equivalent of Flipkart's Dispatch SLA and Amazon's Late Dispatch Rate.
- The **Next Day Dispatch (NDD)** program rewards consistently fast-shipping sellers with increased buyer visibility, per Meesho's own supplier-facing claims.
- Silver/Gold seller tiers require meeting volume thresholds (e.g., 6,000+ units or ₹50 lakh sales in 90 days) alongside low cancellation (<0.15%) and low dispatch-delay rates.
- Sellers can appeal wrongful late-dispatch or cancellation penalties via Supplier Panel > Penalties & Disputes, submitting the Sub Order ID and a stamped manifest proving on-time handover to the courier; verified courier-side delays result in penalty reversal.
