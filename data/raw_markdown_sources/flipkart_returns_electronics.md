# Flipkart Return Policy — Electronics Category Edge Case

## Why Electronics Are Handled Differently
Electronics carry a materially different return process than general merchandise on Flipkart, reflecting both higher fraud risk and brand-service-center obligations. This document isolates the electronics-specific rules referenced in the general returns policy.

## Return vs. Replacement Distinction
Many branded electronics (mobiles, laptops, cameras, tablets, smartwatches) are **replacement-only**, not refund-eligible, once delivered without a manufacturing defect. A refund is issued instead of a replacement only if the seller lacks the exact same product in stock.

## Brand Exclusions on the General Policy
Flipkart's return policy explicitly excludes several major electronics brands from its general/default return terms (e.g., Apple/Beats, Google, Samsung, Realme, JBL, Epson, HP, Dell, Canon, MI products including tablets, laptops, and smartwatches) — these brands' own warranty/service terms govern instead, and sellers should not assume Flipkart's general refund default applies.

## Defect Verification Process
1. Customer reports a defect within the return window.
2. Flipkart or the brand may attempt remote troubleshooting (online tools or phone).
3. If no defect is confirmed or diagnosed within 7 days of delivery, the customer is redirected to the brand's authorized service center rather than continuing through Flipkart's own return flow.
4. Data-bearing devices must have all personal information erased and be reset to factory settings before return, per standard e-commerce practice (this applies across platforms, not uniquely to Flipkart).

## Practical Implication for Sellers
Sellers listing in Electronics should set customer expectations explicitly on the product page (repair-first vs. immediate replacement), since the shorter, replacement-oriented window is a common source of buyer disputes and is exactly the kind of nuance a general "10–30 day return" assumption gets wrong.
