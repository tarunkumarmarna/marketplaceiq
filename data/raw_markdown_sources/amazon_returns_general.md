# Amazon India Return & Refund Policy — General

## Standard Return Window
Amazon.in's general rule is a 30-day return window following the Estimated Delivery Date (EDD) — not from the date the item is actually received, but from the EDD. The exact window and eligibility for a specific item is shown on that item's own product detail page and should be treated as the operative rule, since category-specific windows override the 30-day default (commonly shown as "10 Days Returnable," "7 Days Return & Exchange," or "Non-Returnable" directly on the listing).

## Non-Returnable Categories
Some products are entirely non-returnable (varies by category and, in some cases, by discount/clearance status). International-customer orders are generally not return-eligible at all, though refunds may still be claimed within 5 business days of delivery via customer service.

## Condition Requirements
Items must be returned in original/unused condition with tags, hygiene seals, manuals, accessories, and certificates of authenticity intact. For electronics, any personal data must be erased before return.

## Refund Timelines
- Amazon initiates a refund only after the returned item is received and processed at the fulfillment center (for company-fulfilled items) or after the seller confirms receipt (for seller-fulfilled items).
- Refund requests are typically processed within 13 days of successful pickup.
- Actual credit to the customer's account/bank takes an additional few business days depending on payment method (COD refunds go via NEFT to a bank account or as Amazon Pay balance).

## A-to-Z Guarantee (Third-Party Seller Disputes)
For orders sold and fulfilled by a third-party seller, customers can escalate to Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee if the seller doesn't resolve an issue — this generally requires requesting the return within 30 days of delivery and returning the item within 14 days of initiating the return through the Online Returns Center. This is Amazon's built-in dispute-resolution safety net specifically for third-party marketplace transactions, distinct from returns on items Amazon sells and ships itself.
